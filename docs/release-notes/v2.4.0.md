# AMPM Viewer 2.4.0

Plots of one point per part, with trend lines, marker shapes, point labels and
error bars on X; exports from every row; pictures in Help.

**Download**

- **Windows:** `ampm-viewer-v2.4.0-x64-setup.exe`. Run it to install, or to
  upgrade an earlier version in place (your settings, macros and plots are kept).
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `ampm-viewer-v2.4.0-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag
  AMPM Viewer to Applications, replacing the old one.

### Added

- **One point per part on a 2D Scatter.** **One point per** in the New plot
  dialog draws a point for each part (or each specimen, or any column's
  values) instead of each row. Beside X and Y, choose what the point shows of
  that part's rows: mean, median, SD, CoV (%), min or max. So a measurement
  brought in from Excel (a distortion, a density) against each part's mean or
  CoV melt pool is one plot. The axes say what they show (*Mean of Meltpool*),
  and the hover gives the part, its X and Y and how many rows they come from.
- **Trend line** on 2D Scatter and Mean ± SD plots, from the dialog or the
  toolbar: the least-squares line through the points, labelled in a corner
  with its equation, R² and p. Double-click the label to change its font,
  colour and corner, what it shows, and the line's colour, width and dashes.
- **Shape by**: each value of a column in its own marker shape (disc, square,
  triangle, ...), with the shapes in the legend. Colour and shape can show
  two settings at once: colour by scan length, shape by build orientation.
- **Label points**: a column's value written beside each point (a specimen's
  name, say), for up to 300 points. Double-click a label to set the font,
  size, colour and side; the labels keep their distance from the points as
  you zoom.
- **Error bars on X** for Mean ± SD plots (**On X too** in the dialog, or the
  toolbar): X's mean ± its spread over each group's parts, as SD, SE or 95% CI
  like the vertical bars.
- **Pictures in Help.** Articles show screenshots, framed and captioned, in
  dark or light to match the app; click one to see it full size. New
  articles: *Exporting a build's data* (DataHUB Generator step by step, and
  the parts CSV and STL from QuantAM) and *Installing and uninstalling*.
  *Welcome*, *Opening a build* and *Excel template* gained pictures.

### Fixed

- **Export to Excel uses every row.** On a build of more than 1,000,000 rows,
  an *All layers* export (and a summary's means and CoVs) came from an evenly
  spread sample, as plots do. Exports now read every row. A raw export with
  more rows than an Excel sheet holds is refused with what to do instead.

### First start

Neither installer is signed yet. On Windows, if SmartScreen stops the setup,
choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**; if it only offers **Don't run**,
right-click the setup, choose **Properties**, tick **Unblock**, click **OK**
and run it again. On a Mac, the first time: macOS 15 and later, open AMPM
Viewer, close the warning, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > **Open
Anyway**; macOS 14 and earlier, Control-click AMPM Viewer in Applications and
choose **Open**.
