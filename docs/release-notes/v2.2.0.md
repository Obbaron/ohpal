# AMPM Viewer 2.2.0

A Mean ± SD plot for measurements such as Archimedes density, axis ranges and
log scales, and toolbar group names that line up with their buttons.

**Download**

- **Windows:** `ampm-viewer-v2.2.0-x64-setup.exe`. Run it to install, or to
  upgrade an earlier version in place (your settings, macros and plots are kept).
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `ampm-viewer-v2.2.0-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag
  AMPM Viewer to Applications, replacing the old one.

### What's new

- **Mean ± SD plot** (New plot ▸ Mean ± SD). One point per part at the mean of
  a measurement and its repeats, with error bars, against a process setting.
  For example, Archimedes density against energy density:
  1. Fill in Density (1), (2) and (3) on the samples template.
  2. Bring them in with Add column ▸ Excel.
  3. Add a calculation `[Hatches Power] * 1000 / [Hatch Speed]`.
  4. Plot.

  Error bars show the sample SD (as Excel's STDEV.S), the standard error or a
  95% confidence interval, switched from the toolbar. Hover a point for its
  mean, SD and n.
- **Axis range and log scale.** Click an axis and open it in the side panel to
  set its **Minimum** and **Maximum**, or put it on a **Log scale** (2D scatter
  and Mean ± SD).
- **Toolbar.** Each group's name now sits centred under its buttons, with the
  separators between groups. In a narrow window, the **»** at the right opens a
  menu of the groups that don't fit. Before, it did nothing.
- Renaming a column now also renames it in a plot's "group by" (KDE, CoV
  summary).

### First start

Neither installer is signed yet. On Windows, if SmartScreen stops the setup,
choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. On a Mac, the first time: macOS 15
and later, open AMPM Viewer, close the warning, then System Settings > Privacy &
Security > **Open Anyway**; macOS 14 and earlier, Control-click AMPM Viewer in
Applications and choose **Open**.
